Frequently Asked Questions

Still unsure? Here's what most attendees ask before registering.

What's included in my conference pass? +

Every pass includes access to keynotes and technical talks, workshop seating based on your tier, networking sessions, and a digital badge for the event.

Can I get a refund if I can't attend? +

Yes. Full refunds are available up to 30 days before the event. After that, your pass can be transferred to a colleague at no extra cost.

Is there a virtual attendance option? +

DevConf 2026 is an in-person only event this year. Recordings of all main-stage talks are included with Standard, Pro, and Team passes.

Are meals included in the ticket price? +

Yes, networking lunch and coffee breaks are included for all three days. The Pro tier also includes a VIP networking dinner.

How do I lock in the early-bird price? +

Simply complete your registration and payment before August 15, 2026. Your pass tier and price are locked in the moment payment is confirmed.
